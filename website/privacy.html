Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Vertex Revenue books sales calls for US business-to-business firms. This page explains what information we handle, why, and what you can ask us to do with it. If anything here is unclear, email deepansh.vertexrevenue@gmail.com.

Who we are

Information about prospects we contact

  • We only use business contact details that companies publish themselves, such as a name, role, company and a publicly listed work email.
  • We never buy contact lists and never guess email addresses.
  • We use these details only to send a short, relevant business email and to follow up once.
  • Every email includes our postal address and a way to opt out. Opt-outs are honoured permanently.
  • When a prospect agrees to a call, we share their name, company, role and the answers they gave us with the one client firm they agreed to speak with.

Information about our clients

When you book a call or work with us, we keep your name, company, contact details, calendar booking details and the notes we need to run your campaign. We use them only to provide the service and to contact you about it.

This website

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies and does not run analytics that identify you. Bookings are handled by Cal.com, which has its own privacy policy.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information to anyone. We share it only as described above, or if the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep information only as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected for. You can ask us at any time to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Email us and we will act on it within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
