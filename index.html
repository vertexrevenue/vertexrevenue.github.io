The questions we ask before booking

We talk to business owners moving from stability to growth, aiming for ₹10 to 50L a year. Anyone who doesn't pass a question doesn't move forward.

"Are you trying to scale the business right now, or mostly keeping things stable?"

Continues if they're trying to scale, growth has slowed, or they're stuck at the same level.

"What's the main thing holding growth back: leads, systems, or clarity?"

Tells us whether the problem is one you actually solve.

"Have you invested in coaching, consulting or tools to grow before?"

Continues if they saw value and are open to investing again. Stops if they've never invested and won't, or call every past investment a waste.

"This sounds worth a proper conversation. Want me to set up a call with a provider I work with?"

If yes, the call goes on your calendar. That's the only way a call gets booked.
