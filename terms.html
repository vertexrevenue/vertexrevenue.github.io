Terms of service

Last updated 18 November 2026

These are the plain-language terms for working with Vertex Revenue. When we start working together, we confirm them by email, together with anything specific to your campaign.

The service

We research US prospects that match the ideal buyer we agree with you, contact them by email as Vertex Revenue, qualify interested replies against four checks (a real problem, budget, authority and an explicit yes to a call), and book qualified calls on your calendar with a call brief. We send a short report every Friday.

What counts as a booked call

A call counts only when it is scheduled for a specific time, confirmed by the prospect, and accepted on your calendar.

Replacement policy

If a booked call turns out to miss any of the four checks, tell us after the call. It does not count, and we replace it at no extra cost.

What we do not promise

We do not guarantee a number of calls, closed deals or revenue. Results depend on your market, your offer and your sales process. We agree a realistic target with you in week 1 and report against it honestly.

Price and payment

$500 per month, paid monthly in advance. No setup fee and no per-call charges.

Cancelling

The service is month-to-month. You can cancel at any time by email, with no notice period and no exit fee. Your last paid month runs to its end.

Your part

A 45-minute onboarding call, a booking link, sign-off on the screening script, and someone from your firm attending the calls that are booked.

Outreach standards

We follow the US CAN-SPAM Act: honest subject lines, a real postal address and a working opt-out in every email. We never use your domain or inbox for cold email.
